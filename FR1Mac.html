Record or import audio on Mac. FR-1 finds music and speech, transcribes speech, and keeps clips ready to review.

Download for Mac (Universal Binary) Version 1.0

Record and analyze on Mac

Capture a session, then let FR-1 detect music and speech and transcribe spoken parts on your Mac.

Find and organize recordings

Search titles, clip names, and transcripts. Use folders, rename clips, and recover recordings from Recently Deleted.

Listen your way

Keep playback close while browsing recordings. Variable playback speeds beyond 1x are included with FR-1 Pro.

Bring in audio

Import files from Finder or the File menu, then analyze them alongside your Mac recordings. Import requires FR-1 Pro.

Export clips and sessions

Export a single clip or a full recording for free. Batch clip audio, tune lists, and transcript exports require FR-1 Pro.

Optional iCloud Sync

Keep recordings and folders in sync with your iPhone and iPad. Sync requires FR-1 Pro; your local library stays on Mac.

FR-1 for Mac works on its own for recording, organizing, playback, and basic exports. FR-1 Pro unlocks audio import, iCloud Sync, starred clips, faster playback, and batch or text exports.

System Requirements

  • macOS 15 or later
  • Speech analysis uses the available on-device speech recognition on your Mac
  • iCloud Sync requires FR-1 Pro and the same iCloud account on your devices

Version History

1.0 — Initial release

Privacy

FR-1 for Mac does not collect app usage data. Recording analysis and transcription run on your Mac. If you enable iCloud Sync, recordings and library changes sync through your private iCloud account. Settings are saved on your device.
